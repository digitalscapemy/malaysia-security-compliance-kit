## 1. Roles & subject matter
The Controller determines the purposes and means of processing. The Processor processes personal
data **only on documented instructions** from the Controller, for: `[describe the service]`.

## 2. Categories of data & subjects
- Data subjects: `[e.g. client's customers, employees]`
- Personal data: `[e.g. name, email, phone]`
- **Sensitive personal data** (if any, incl. biometric): `[list / none]`
- Duration: for the term of the main agreement.

## 3. Processor obligations (PDPA 2024)
The Processor shall:
- a. Process only on the Controller's instructions; not for its own purposes.
- b. Take **practical security steps** (technical + organisational) to protect data from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, alteration, destruction — referencing the controls in this kit (`02`–`05`).
- c. Ensure personnel are bound by confidentiality.
- d. Assist the Controller with data-subject rights (access, correction, **portability**) and with breach handling.
- e. **Notify the Controller of a personal data breach without undue delay** (target: within `[24]` hours of discovery) so the Controller can meet the 72-hour Commissioner deadline.
- f. On termination, **delete or return** all personal data per the Controller's choice and the Retention Policy.
- g. Make available information needed to demonstrate compliance and allow **audits**.

## 4. Sub-processors
- [ ] Controller authorises the sub-processors listed in the attached **Sub-Processor Register**.
- [ ] Processor gives `[14]` days' notice of any new sub-processor; Controller may object.
- [ ] Each sub-processor is bound by equivalent obligations.

## 5. Cross-border transfer
Personal data may be processed in `[country/region]` via `[cloud provider]`. The Processor ensures
the destination provides protection comparable to the PDPA, per the 2024 cross-border guidelines.

## 6. Security measures (summary)
Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest; access control + MFA; audit logging; tested backups;
vulnerability scanning; incident response. Detail per the Security Policy.

## 7. Breach notification
Processor → Controller within `[24]` hours of discovery, with: nature, data/subjects affected,
likely consequences, measures taken. Parties cooperate on Commissioner + data-subject notices.

## 8. Liability
Liability is subject to the **limitation of liability** in the main agreement `[REF]`. Each party
indemnifies the other for losses caused by its own breach of this DPA. `[Insert cap.]`

## 9. Term & termination
This DPA runs with the main agreement. On termination, clause 3(f) applies.
